Frequently Asked Questions

What is Ki-Liner?

Ki-Liner is a discreet, ultra-thin liner designed to sit at the bottom of a cup and add an extra layer of protection in social environments without changing the drinking experience.

How does Ki-Liner work?

Ki-Liner is designed to interact at the surface level within the drink environment, helping to disrupt or limit unwanted alterations while remaining invisible to the user.

Will Ki-Liner affect the taste or smell of my drink?

No. Ki-Liner is engineered to be neutral, meaning it does not change the taste, smell, or appearance of your drink.

Is Ki-Liner visible inside the cup?

Ki-Liner is transparent and sits flat at the bottom of the cup, making it virtually unnoticeable during normal use.

Who can use Ki-Liner?

Ki-Liner is designed for individuals, bars, nightclubs, event organizers, and institutions looking to add an extra layer of safety in social settings.

Can I order Ki-Liner in bulk for my business or event?

Yes. Secura Labs offers bulk ordering and partnership opportunities for venues, organizations, and distributors. You can contact us directly for custom pricing.

What substances does it detect?

Ki-Liner is designed around chemical signatures associated with common drink-adulteration concerns, including GHB, ketamine analogs, and benzodiazepines. Confirmed coverage should be reviewed through SecuraLabs validation materials during rollout.

What does it not detect?

Ki-Liner is not positioned as a universal detector for every contaminant, drug, or unsafe condition. Venues should review confirmed detection coverage, use conditions, and limitations before deployment.

Is food-safety documentation available?

Food-safety and regulatory documentation should be reviewed as part of commercial or institutional deployment. SecuraLabs can provide the appropriate status and materials during partnership discussions.

Can it create false positives?

Any detection system can have edge cases. SecuraLabs development focuses on minimizing false positives across complex beverage compositions and optimizing reaction time.

Is Ki-Liner single-use?

Yes. Ki-Liner is a single-use, disposable liner that can be integrated into hospitality settings using standard cup formats.
